About Me

As a Postdoctoral Scholar at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) and the United States Geological Survey (USGS), I specialize in developing innovative robotics and machine learning solutions for earth science applications, including earthquake sciences, geological mapping, and hazard assessment.

Research Excellence

  • Core Expertise: Robotics and Machine Learning for Geosciences
  • Applied Research: Natural Hazards, Fault Zone Characterization, Remote Sensing
  • Technical Innovation: Autonomous UAV Systems, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, 3D Mapping
  • Impact Areas: Earthquake Science, Geological Field Mapping, Hazard Assessment
