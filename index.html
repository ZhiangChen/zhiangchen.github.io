About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at California Institute of Technology (Caltech) and United States Geological Survey, specializing in Robotics and Machine Learning for Geosciences. My work focuses on developing autonomous systems for natural hazard assessment, fault zone characterization, and remote sensing applications.

Research Interests

  • Primary Focus: Robotics and Machine Learning for Geosciences
  • Secondary Areas: Natural Hazards, Fault Zone Characterization, Remote Sensing
  • Methodologies: UAV Systems, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Geospatial Analysis
  • Applications: Earthquake Science, Geological Mapping, Environmental Monitoring

Education

  • PhD: Exploration Systems Design (Sensor Networks) - Arizona State University (2022)
  • Master's: Mechanical Engineering (Robotics) - Case Western Reserve University (2017)
  • Bachelor's: Mechanical Engineering (Mechatronics) - Central South University (2015)

Current Position

Postdoctoral Scholar at California Institute of Technology & United States Geological Survey
