Research Projects

Explore my research projects and ongoing work

Seismic Hazard Analysis: Precariously Balanced Rocks

Investigating the dynamics of precariously balanced rocks (PBRs) at Centennial Bluffs using autonomous mapping and physics engine simulations to understand the impacts of earthquakes on different large-scale distributions of fragile geological features and constrain seismic hazard assessments.

Read More

Earthquake Surface Ruptures

Developing a multi-UAV system to enhance the mapping of surface fractures in fault zones by providing high-resolution (5 mm) data over large areas, with applications in the Southern San Andreas Fault near the Salton Sea and rapid response mapping for earthquake events.

Read More

Post-wildfire Debris Flows

Studying the initiation and dynamics of debris flows following wildfire events using high-resolution topographic data and machine learning approaches to better predict and mitigate post-fire hazards in vulnerable communities.

Read More

Sediment Transport in Tectonics: Rocky Fault Scarps

Analyzing sediment transport processes along rocky fault scarps to understand the relationship between tectonic activity and landscape evolution, using field observations and numerical modeling to quantify erosion and deposition patterns.

Read More

Tornados

Investigating tornado formation, dynamics, and damage patterns using advanced remote sensing techniques and computational fluid dynamics models to improve understanding of severe weather phenomena and enhance early warning systems for tornado-prone regions.
