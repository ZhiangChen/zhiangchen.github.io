Geologic Fault Zone Mapping

Developing a multi-UAV system to enhance the mapping of surface fractures in fault zones by providing high-resolution (5 mm) data over large areas, with an initial application in the Southern San Andreas Fault near the Salton Sea.

Precariously Balanced Rocks

Investigating the dynamics of PBRs at Centennial Bluffs using autonomous mapping and physics engine simulations, to understand the impacts of earthquakes on different large-scale distributions of fragile geological features.
