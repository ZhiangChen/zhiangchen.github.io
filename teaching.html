Publications with Students

Virtual Shake Robot 2: Benchmarking Physics Engine for Overturning Precariously Balanced Rocks and Constraining Local Ground Motions

Chen, Z., Mahalle, A., Saifullah, K., Das, J., Wittich, C., Kottke, A., Madugo, C., & Arrowsmith, R.

Rock Mechanics and Rock Engineering, 2026 (In Preparation)

Benchmarking physics engines for accurate simulation of precariously balanced rock overturning dynamics and constraining local ground motion parameters. Student co-author Akshay Mahalle contributed to the physics engine evaluation and simulation validation.

Shakebot: A Low-cost, Open-sourced Shake Table for Earthquake Research and Education

Chen, Z., Keating, D., Shethwala, Y., Pandian Saravanakumaran, A., Arrowsmith, R., Kottke, A., Wittich, C., & Das, J.

2024 IEEE International Conference on Automation Science and Engineering, 2024

Development of a low-cost, open-source shake table for earthquake research and education applications. Student co-authors Yash Shethwala and Aravind Adhith Pandian Saravanakumaran contributed significantly to the mechanical design, assembly, and testing of the shake table system.
